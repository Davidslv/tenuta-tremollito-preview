Meet Malpelo

Eight wines, named after the people of Giovanni Verga, whose home estate is our neighbour.

They all come from one hillside, 620 metres up, beside a spent volcano.

Our neighbour, Verga

Giovanni Verga was born in Catania and grew up in Vizzini, where his parents farmed citrus groves and vineyards. His family’s estate is next to ours. He became the great writer of Italian verismo, and he set his stories here, in the Sicily he was born in. Each of our wines carries one of his characters.

Our story

After three decades in London, back to Sicily: an old farmhouse, and a vineyard everyone had forgotten.

Vincenzo Giordano and Margherita Palmieri restored both. The vineyard sits at 620 metres beside Monte Altore, a spent volcano, facing south-east. Native Sicilian vines, unirrigated for more than a decade. Every grape is picked by hand. First harvest, 2011.

Organic, certified by ICEA, a control body authorised by the Italian Ministry of Agriculture. No synthetic chemicals, no GMOs.

The place

The estate lies in the valley of the Dirillo river, near Licodia Eubea, an ancient town ringed by archaeological sites. Poplars, tamarisks and dwarf palms grow around the lake below the vines.
